**Known difference:** DSH plugin's `followup` commits a model-visible user-role notice to session log, hidden as a human bubble by this TUI. Pi's internal retry cue and atomic sibling branch are not available at this DSH extension seam. Do not claim exact one-to-one parity. `/retry` after aborted tool activity could prompt model to repeat side effects; verify workspace before actions.

`@syncended/dsh-retry` 0.2.3: MIT and APIs (`agent/request-error`, `llm/retry`, `sessionProjections`) are available in pinned runtime, but it handles automatic provider failures and crash-resume only, not `/retry` or Pi selected branch. Its default `maxRetries=2` and generic `PI_AI_ERROR` retries could bypass user exclusions unless wrapped. No code reused.

**Status:** 6 ported, 2 built-in, 4 gaps. Full one-to-one parity is not available without new core/TUI seams.
